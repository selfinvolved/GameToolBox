eDPI Calculator

eDPI combines your mouse DPI and in-game sensitivity into one number. It's useful for comparing settings within the same game.

Your eDPI
960

800 DPI × 1.2 sensitivity = 960 eDPI. Compare this only with players using the same game's sensitivity scale.

This calculator does not tell you what sensitivity is best. Comfort and consistency matter more than copying a number.
